## What the library does that the app does not

- First round from a seeding list (#6).
- Refuses more heats than boats (#3), an entry after the split (#22), and building a round in a one-heat event at the handler level (#26).
- Accepts a late entry into a heat that has already raced, including a one-fleet event in mid-series (#16). This is wider than the app, and probably not wanted.
- Allows removing an entry that never raced after racing has started (#23). The app refuses any removal after the first race.
- Accepts an empty heat in a pre-assigned round. The app is stuck: the round can never be completed.
- Undo by taking a decision out of the log, with every later decision that depended on it re-checked and refused.
- `nextStep` covers `enterBoats` and `createHeats`.

## Most important gaps, ranked by risk of wrong results

1. **#23 Library bug: a removed boat stays in a final fleet.** `boatRemoved` after the split only cleans the qualifying heats, leaving a boat that is not entered in the fleet. That changes the SHRS 5.2 largest heat (every no-place score in the Final) and makes every fleet sheet require her. Fix: refuse `boatRemoved` once the fleets are made.
2. **#38 Who decided a DSQ (SHRS 3.1(5)).** The app freezes heat assignments for every leaderboard DSQ/DNE/DGM/DPI/RDG edit. If migration maps those edits to `raceCorrected` instead of `protestDecided`, the next-round heats come out different. Fix: map edits with those codes to `protestDecided`, the same way the app treats them.
3. **#53 Boats sharing a place at a fleet boundary.** App: `boat_id` order. Library: entry order. This can put a different boat in Gold or Silver. Fix: agree on one order and state it.
4. **#16 Late entry into a heat that has raced.** The library accepts it, the app refuses it. Results and movement then differ (she is DNC in that race and moves as DNC). Fix: refuse it in `assign` when `heat.races.length > 0`, or make it a deliberate rule.
5. **#29(b) RDG/DPI entered at race entry move boats in the app** (RDG sorts as a finisher at heat size + 1; DPI sorts as a no-place). In the library they never move a boat. Fix: confirm whether the entry UI allows these codes; if so, map them to `protestDecided`.
6. **#30 / #68 Empty heats.** In progressive mode the library throws `TypeError` from `replay`, which is a crash, not a refusal. The app gets stuck instead. Fix: skip or refuse empty heats in `createRound`.
7. **#46 Final split with a built but unsailed round.** The app allows it, the library refuses it. Existing app events in this state cannot be replayed as they are. Fix: drop the trailing `roundCreated` when converting, or let `startFinal` ignore a round with no races.
8. **#41/#42 Transfers are missing** from the library. The app can only do them before the first race. Fix: a `boatMoved` decision with the app's checks.
9. **#22, #8 Migration refusals.** An entry made after the split, or sail numbers like "012" next to "12", are accepted by the app but refused by the library, so replay stops at that decision.
10. **#51 Oversize confirm** (the app UI must confirm before logging `allow-oversize`) and **#26 one-heat round** (the app handler should refuse). Both are low risk, because the UI already guards them.
