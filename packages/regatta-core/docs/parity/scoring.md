# Parity inventory: race scoring, corrections, protest/redress/penalty codes, discards, standings, tie-breaks, explanations

Scope: ScoringApp `core/scoring-library` (`app:` = the repository root) against regatta-core (`core:` = `packages/regatta-core/`). `packages/regatta-core/src` in the app repo matches the standalone repo apart from `cli.ts`. All claims below were checked in the code on both sides unless marked UNVERIFIED.

How the two sides model things. The app stores place numbers and points in `Scores` and keeps derived standings tables, rebuilt on every read. The library gets an ordered sheet (`RaceLine[]`, with `tied`, `code` and `penalty` per line), keeps protest decisions as separate overlays, and computes everything on read.

## 3. Most important gaps, ranked by risk of wrong results

1. **Undo and decision removal with positional race refs.** Library `RaceRef.race` is an index. Removing a `raceScored` that is not the last for its heat silently shifts every later reference to that heat (corrections, protests, RDG2). Removing the last one makes replay refuse the decisions that reference it, which the app drops without a word (and the app keeps stale RDG2 averages). _Fix line: the adapter may only undo the last race of a heat, and must remove its dependent decisions in the same step._
2. **Who decided: race office vs protest committee.** The app never recorded it, and after a DSQ promotion the stored positions no longer contain the original sheet (DSQ boat at heat + 1). Migrating existing events into a log, and mapping leaderboard edits, needs a rule. Points are the same either way; 3.1(5) heat assignment is not. _Fix line: map DSQ/DNE/DGM/RDG_/DPI leaderboard edits to `protestDecided`, everything else to `raceCorrected`, and import legacy races as a sheet rebuilt from position order.\*
3. **"No heat" DNC handling.** (a) The app files the DNC under the earliest-scored heat's race, the library under heat A. This changes SHRS 5.7(ii) results when heats are not scored in A, B, C order, and the differential test never covers that case. (b) The library cannot redress, recode or correct such a DNC (RDG for a late entry's missed races); the app can. _Fix line: pick one anchor rule in both and add a decision for scores outside a heat._
4. **Late boat assigned into a heat that already raced.** The app gives DNS in her own heat's race; the library gives DNC anchored to heat A, and then requires her on any later `raceCorrected` of that earlier race. Points are equal, but tie-break and code differ. _Fix line: let `checkLines` use the heat's boats as of that race._
5. **RDG1/RDG2 with nothing to average, or an RDG2 basis lost.** The app keeps a frozen value (stale after heat-size changes or undo); the library recomputes to largest heat + 1. _Fix line: decide which is right and align._
6. **Missing input validation in the library.** No range check on DPI/RDG3 points (the app requires > 0 and ≤ 99), no check of custom or threshold discard profiles, no runtime check of codes (only via the schema), RAF not mapped. _Fix line: add the app's checks to `replay` / settings._
7. **Display-level differences the screens will show:** unraced fleet ordered by qualifying total and boat_id (app) vs qualifying place (library); final-only export ranks by overall order; which equal worst score is bracketed; race column order when a round sails 2+ races; order of boats sharing a place. Totals and places otherwise agree.
8. **Discard lock after undo.** The app stays locked; the library unlocks. Low risk.
9. **Score and tie-break explanations missing in the library.** A feature gap, not a wrong result. The app's explanation currently reads `Leaderboard`/`FinalLeaderboard` and Scores directly.

Notes on the library README's "Checked against the app" list: items 1 (non-transitive 5.7 sort) and 2 (redress beside a tie) do not match the current app code (`app:src/shared/scoring/ranking.ts:81-99`, `app:src/shared/raceColumnEdit.ts:109-135`). The app now does what the library does, apart from the `circular` flag. Item 3 (who decided) still holds. Whether the README predates these app changes is UNVERIFIED. The differential test does not exercise RDG2, undo, out-of-order heat scoring, 2+ races per multi-heat round, protests on non-finishers, or corrections after a protest on the same race (`app:src/__tests__/regattaCore.differential.integration.test.ts:241-348, 577-586`).
